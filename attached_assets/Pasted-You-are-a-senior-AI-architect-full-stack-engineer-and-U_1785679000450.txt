You are a senior AI architect, full-stack engineer, and UI/UX designer.

# Product Name
BrandFlow

# Tagline
Create. Schedule. Engage. Grow.

# Product Overview

BrandFlow is an AI-powered social media manager that acts as a company's autonomous marketing assistant. Instead of simply generating content, it manages the complete social media workflow from content creation to audience engagement.

The platform should feel modern, premium, and AI-first.
